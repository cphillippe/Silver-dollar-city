# Night Watch seat taps at 375×667

Viewport taps for an Easy night on a 375×667 phone. Each one was clicked in the live prove and the lamp’s feet landed on the plate point below.

The board sits under the top bar, so these are viewport pixels, not plate units. Choose the tool, then tap. A fresh night is `?playtest=1`, then Silver City. `nwRound` is the round number (round 10 is `nwRound=10`).
